Tidewatch 0.4.2

New in this release

  • Settings window. Open it with the gear next to the agent count, Settings… in the menu bar (⌘,) or a right-click on the widget. The widget shows every change at once.
  • Blur and Tint sliders for the glass.
  • Appearance: System, Light or Dark. Dark glass reads well over dark pages even when macOS is light.
  • Text contrast: a soft halo under the widget's text, so it reads over any background.
  • Review lamp fades: after 5, 10 or 30 minutes, or never until you open the session.
  • The status is always shown in full. When a row is short on room, the project name gives way and keeps its beginning.
  • A wider panel, so longer conversation titles fit.

Install

Already on 0.3.0 or later? Tidewatch offers this version on its own, or choose Check for Updates… in the menu bar. New here? Open Tidewatch-0.4.2.dmg, drag Tidewatch to Applications, start it and choose Connect to Claude Code in the menu bar.

Requires macOS 14.4+ on Apple silicon.
