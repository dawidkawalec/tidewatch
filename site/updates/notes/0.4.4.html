Tidewatch 0.4.4

New in this release

  • Latest activity on top. Sessions are sorted by their last status change (started working, asked for your approval, finished), and windows by their latest session, so what needs you sits at the top. A working session does not jump on every tool call, and a tab keeps its "#n" name when rows move.
  • Every session visible. The widget grows to the height of your screen: the lamp column and the list show all sessions without scrolling. Only more sessions than the screen holds fall back to scrolling.
  • No halo around the glass. The soft shadow under the column and the panel is gone; it showed as a blurred band around them.

Install

Already on 0.3.0 or later? Tidewatch offers this version on its own, or choose Check for Updates… in the menu bar. New here? Open Tidewatch-0.4.4.dmg, drag Tidewatch to Applications, start it and choose Connect to Claude Code in the menu bar.

Requires macOS 14.4+ on Apple silicon.
